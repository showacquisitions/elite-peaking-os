🔒 Privacy

Privacy and data

What is stored and where
  1. On this device: your profile, plan, logs, recipes and food entries are saved in this browser. Progress photos are saved here too and stay here unless you choose to share them with your coach.
  2. If you create an account: your email and your progress data are stored online with Supabase so they sync between your devices. If you connect Apple Health, the daily health numbers you send are stored there too. If you turn on notifications, your device's push address is stored so reminders can reach you. If you share photos with a coach, a copy is kept in private storage that only you and that coach can open, and turning sharing off deletes it. The data is private to your account from other users, but it is not end-to-end encrypted, so the person who runs this app's database can technically read it.
  3. Food search: the words or barcode you search are sent to Open Food Facts, which can see your network address. Nothing else is sent to them.
  4. Hosting: the page is served by GitHub Pages, which logs ordinary page requests.
  5. No ads, no analytics, no tracking scripts, and your data is never sold.
  6. Your control: "Export backup" downloads your data. "Delete my account and cloud data" in the sync card removes the online copy. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes the device copy and photos.
  7. Not medical advice. Plans are general estimates. Check with a doctor before big changes to training or diet, especially with a medical condition, injury or pregnancy.
